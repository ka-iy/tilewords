Grateful acknowledgement is made to the authors and maintainers of the
sources below, whose work makes the game and its definitions possible.

Definitions are shown for reference during play. Where more than one
source defines a word, the Wiktionary sense is preferred; Webster's 1913,
WordNet, and the glossaries fill gaps for archaic and dialectal words.
