==============================
  ABOUT
==============================

TileWords is a Scrabble®-like game where the player competes with the machine to use individual letters to construct cromulent words on a game board to gain the endorphin hit from watching those sweet sweet points _racking_ (heh heh) up.

TileWords is developed by Kartikeya IYER and is available (including source code) from:
https://github.com/ka-iy/tilewords

TileWords uses public-domain and libre/freely available word lists and definitions.
No proprietary lexicons were harmed during the making of this game.

Definitions are shown for reference during play. Where more than one source defines a word, the Wiktionary sense is preferred; Webster's 1913, WordNet, and the glossaries fill gaps for archaic and dialectal words.

While (almost) every effort has been made to fill the gaps in the definitions, gaps do remain; where a pplayed word has no definition that could be found, such will be indicated in the "Definitions" tab on the game screen.
